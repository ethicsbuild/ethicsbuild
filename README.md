# Gage Cass Woodle
**Founder & Builder | Human-First AI | Two Decades in Experience & Nonprofit Leadership**

I build human-first systems for trust and AI, drawing on two decades in immersive production and nonprofit leadership. I founded and ran Mythic Bridge (an NYC nonprofit, 11 years; our students' short film was a New York Emmy nominee, on which I served as executive producer), produced large-scale live experiences, and now design AI frameworks centered on human judgment and agency.

A note on status: every project below is labeled at its true stage. I would rather underclaim than oversell.
